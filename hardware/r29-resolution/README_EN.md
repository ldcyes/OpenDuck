# R29: R26 assembly interference and dynamics review

[简体中文](README.md) · [R28 findings](../r28-r26-interference/README_EN.md) · [R26 assembly](../r26-cover-first/README_EN.md)

R29 is an **engineering candidate without a built prototype**. It keeps the R26 assembly's 1,224 installed items and 15 joints, replacing one lower head shell and four PEEK wire-exit films. The thigh covers, motors, aluminium harness bridges and mounting transforms stay in place. The [complete selection](verification/assembly_selection.json) and [source-hash mapping](../../provenance/r29-source-mapping.json) identify the files. The [standalone Blender assembly](https://github.com/ldcyes/OpenDuck/releases/download/r29-r26-closure-candidate-20260929/OpenDuck-R29-R26-mass-motion.blend) includes a replay of the four-step simulation recalculated with R29 estimated mass; its [readback](verification/blender_readback.json) checks 1,224 parts, the five changed meshes, and the first and last animated poses.

Bounded searches attempted all 81 combined endpoints; **59** have complete geometric routes and passed 111,125 nearby body checks. | **22 combined poses remain unresolved**. The closest fitted wire pair is only **0.0161 mm** apart. The 59 routes are geometric witnesses, not measured elastic equilibria; continuous transitions, abrasion and retention remain untested. See the [81-pose report](verification/COMBINED81_REVIEW.md) and [first-article plan](verification/HARNESS_REVIEW.md). |
| R26/R29 walking dynamics | The original R26 estimated mass is **4.443060 kg**; the final R29 five-part assembly is **4.442729 kg**. Nominal, half-time-step, μ=0.5 and nine-contact-point cases were recalculated for both. The R29 nominal sequence lasts **195.44 s**, records two forward steps per foot and about **33.38° peak-to-peak body roll**. The central 20–80% of each planned swing has no sampled contact above 2 N. [Summary](verification/R29_dynamics_validation.json) and [contact audit](verification/swing_contact_audit.json). | Passing numerical step and tracking criteria **does not satisfy the current robot's limits**. Left hip roll peaks at **0.85955 Nm**, only **4.49%** below an unmeasured 0.9 Nm design cap. A 15% margin needs at least **1.01124 Nm measured sustained torque** at 10.30–10.35 V, 40°C for 7,200 s. Right hip roll, right knee and left hip pitch need their own qualification. |

**Active motion blocker:** the RK runtime's mouth structural limit is **0.05 Nm**, while the R29 model needs about **0.0567 Nm RMS to hold the mouth closed** and reaches about **0.05925 Nm** across the four numerical cases. The MuJoCo model used a provisional 0.08 Nm cap, so its completed path is not a pass against the active structural limit. A concrete counterbalance/load-path revision and rerun, or a locked-mouth configuration with its own validation, is required. The software limit must not simply be raised. An optional [dual-torsion-spring calculation and coil-space study](studies/mouth-counterbalance/README.md) suggests a reduction path, but the spring legs, anchors and load-bearing installation are not designed or installed in this release.

For fabrication, use the hashed 3MF head shell and STEP PEEK lips, then scan the assembled and loaded clearances and measure mouth overtravel. Verify each of the 31 wire lengths, insulation, 5 N retention, bend radius and continuous motion through the 81 combined head poses; run an initial 10,000-cycle inspection. Measure sustained low-battery actuator capability on a current-limited bench before supported calibration, load tests and floor walking. A failed gate requires revision and re-analysis. R29 grants no manufacturing or physical-walking approval.
