# Conditional mouth torsion-spring study

This is a separate **feasibility calculation**, not a change to the frozen R29 walking evidence or the active **0.05 Nm** mouth structural guard. The unsprung R29 design remains blocked because all four simulated walks require 0.05837–0.05925 Nm peak and roughly 0.05667 Nm RMS from the mouth motor while it holds closed.

A proposed pair of closing torsion springs uses **0.7 mm wire**, **11.5 mm mean coil diameter**, **3 active turns each**, assumed steel modulus **200 GPa**, and assumed pair preload **0.020 Nm** at closed. [Gutekunst's manufacturer handbook, §1.4.4, pp. 20–21](https://www.federnshop.com/download/pdf/Gutekunst-Federn-1x1-2013-E.pdf) gives the torsion-spring moment-rate relation. With these assumptions, the pair's calculated rate is **0.04349638 Nm/rad**. The proposed passive closing torque is `−0.020 − 0.04349638 × mouth_angle_rad` Nm, or **−0.027592 Nm** at 10°.

Subtracting that ideal torque from the *saved* actuator samples of each unsprung R29 walk gives approximately **0.03834 Nm peak** in all four cases, below 0.05 Nm at every saved 20 ms sample. This is an analytical replay, not a closed-loop simulation with spring mass, friction or a real torque limit. The old report's integration-step peak and 20 ms joint bounds can suggest a conditional ~0.03925 Nm upper value **only if** the unsaved integration-step actuator torque stayed negative. That sign has not been proved for every step, so it is not a certification.

With the head at HOME and base level, static mouth motor torque including the hypothetical spring changes from **−0.03612 Nm at 0°** to **−0.02439 Nm at 10°**. A finite static grid samples three positions on each of four permitted head axes, base roll −18.3/0/+18.3°, and mouth 0/2/5/8/10°: **1,215 poses**. Its largest absolute residual is **0.03920 Nm**, at neck pitch +5°, head pitch −15°, all other head angles and base roll 0°, mouth closed. This grid does not prove a continuous combined-pose envelope or moving-head dynamics, and some combined head extremes may be geometrically unqualified.

The [machine-readable calculation](analytical_torque.json) hashes the four frozen R29 traces, bound joint intervals, model/contract, runtime guard and analysis script. The spring wire, modulus and preload remain design assumptions. Coil-only nominal clearance has been preliminarily checked by the mechanical designer, but **spring legs, anchors, screw stack, rated material stress/fatigue, mass/inertia and complete 0–10° CAD clearance are not frozen**. An installed mechanism must be drawn and inspected before a new 0.05 Nm integrated nominal run is credible; the original R29 blocked result remains authoritative until then.
